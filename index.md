# Privacy Policy for Layout Corners
Layout Corners does not collect, store, sell, or share personal information.
The app allows users to open PDF drawings from their device and calculate layout measurements. 
PDF files and measurement data are processed locally on the user's device. 
The app does not upload drawings or measurements to our servers.
If you contact us for support, we may use your email address only to respond to your request.
For privacy questions, contact: rmoskal@cox.net
